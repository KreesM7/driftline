# Community code of conduct

Welcome beginners and people with different languages, backgrounds, and abilities. Critique evidence and code, not people.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate privacy violations, and unauthorized testing are unacceptable. Never share another person's identifiers or device reports without consent.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict repeated abuse, applying decisions consistently. After publication, report concerns through the owner's private contact on their GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive conduct reports. Establish a dedicated private contact before a larger community launch.
